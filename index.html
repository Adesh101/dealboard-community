How to negotiate from evidence

A repeatable process, distilled from comparing nationwide dealer worksheets.

Dealer correspondence

Before signing

  • Match the VIN and window sticker to the car you drove or selected. Confirm odometer, warranty start and remaining mileage on a loaner.
  • Compare the final contract to the dated worksheet. Check selling price, rebates, money factor, residual, term, miles, payment, add-ons and every dollar due at signing.
  • Verify refundable deposits, excess mileage, disposition and wear charges. Ask for a revised contract if any line changed. Keep a copy of the signed agreement.

01 / Define the target

Standardize the quote

Specify exact model, trim, equipment, state, 24/36 months, 10k miles/year and whether the first payment is actually due at signing.

02 / Collect evidence

Request the whole worksheet

MSRP, selling price, taxes, acquisition and dealer fees, incentives, money factor, residual, mileage and all upfront cash. Advertisements are not contracts.

03 / Compare honestly

Normalize every dollar

Include shipping, broker costs and nonrefundable fees. Keep refundable deposits separate and never assume unconfirmed loyalty or conquest rebates.

04 / Check the exact vehicle

Protect the configuration

Verify audio, roof, seats, stock/VIN privately, demo mileage, remaining warranty, lease eligibility and condition. Different equipment means different value.

05 / Negotiate the levers

Ask for line-item changes

Push for dealer discount, applicable incentives, removal of add-ons, eligible tax credits and fair money factor. Do not disclose your private calculation model.

06 / Close the loop

Get the final offer in writing

Verify the final buyer's order, exact tax treatment, all-in payment, due at signing and no extras. Save personal documents privately; publish only redacted deal facts.

Email scripts

Initial comparison request
Hi [Name],

I'm comparing quotes for a [year/model/trim] in [state]. Can you send your best written 24- and 36-month lease figures at 10,000 miles/year, with taxes and fees included?

Please itemize MSRP, selling price before incentives, available rebates, money factor, residual, acquisition/dealer fees, monthly payment and total due at signing (including the first payment). Please also confirm equipment, mileage, lease eligibility and availability.

Email is best. Thanks.
Negotiate without sharing your calculations
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the worksheet. I'm considering another comparable offer, but I like your vehicle and would prefer to make this work.

Can you improve the selling price, confirm all eligible manufacturer incentives and remove optional add-ons? Please send a revised all-in monthly payment and total due at signing for the same term and mileage.

Thanks.
Respectful close-out
Hi [Name],

Thanks again for helping me through my car search and for putting together the options. I've finalized a car, so I won't be moving forward with other offers.

I appreciate your time and help.

Best,
[Your name]
