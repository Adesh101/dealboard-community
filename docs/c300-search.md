# How a Texas GLC search ended with a C 300

The black GLC 300 had a payment I could take seriously. It also had the standard stereo. I had spent enough time listening to different systems to know I would notice that every day. I asked the store for a GLC with Burmester and an opening panoramic roof, and kept looking.

## The change that opened up the search

I had been asking several stores to price Mercedes SUVs. I went back to those same conversations and added the C 300. I could be flexible on the body style. I cared much more about the sound system, the roof and how the cabin felt. I asked for the window sticker or build sheet on the actual car. Ventilated seats were a requirement on the GLC and a preference on the sedan.

That change surfaced a black C 300 AMG Line in Texas with Burmester, the opening roof and ventilated seats. Looking at the sedan gave me a second way to get the features I wanted.

## What I did with the worksheets

- **Made the first payment explicit.** The local C 300 quote had the first payment due at signing. I asked for a revision showing what I would owe that day, with the same 24 months and 10,000 miles a year. I also requested the selling price before incentives, each rebate I qualified for, the money factor, the lender's residual and the Texas tax treatment. That gave the store a precise counteroffer to take back to its manager.
- **Asked distant stores for a delivered price.** One out-of-state SUV needed transport and lacked the opening roof. I stopped negotiating on that vehicle and gave the salesperson the specs for an alternative. For a car that did fit, I wanted the shipping charge in the same comparison as the local offer.
- **Checked the quote's expiration before the drive.** The C 300 quote ran through Sunday. The day I could visit was Monday. I asked whether those numbers would still apply before making the trip.

I leased a local 2026 Mercedes-Benz C 300 AMG Line in November 2026. The interior and the Burmester system are what won me over. For anyone repeating the search, the first thing I would write down is which features I would miss on the drive home. That list made it easy to walk away from a well-priced SUV and recognize the right sedan.
