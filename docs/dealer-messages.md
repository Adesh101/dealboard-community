# Dealer messages

Replace the brackets. Use the same term, annual mileage and cash structure across stores. Keep your budget ceiling in your own notes. These messages are for an individual shopper to send to a store with a specific car.

## Ask for a written quote

**Subject** — Lease quote for [model], stock [stock number]

Hi [name],

I'm interested in stock [number]. Is it still available, and does its window sticker list [required option]?

I'd like a [term]-month lease with [miles] miles a year, registered in [ZIP], with $0 customer down and no trade. Could you send the worksheet showing the monthly payment including tax and the exact amount due at signing?

Please include MSRP, selling price before incentives, each rebate and its qualifications, lender, money factor and residual. I'd also like to see the fees and how tax is handled. [For a distant car: Please quote delivery to my ZIP separately.] I can [pick up/take delivery] by [date] if everything works.

Thanks,
[first name]

## Fill a gap without restarting the negotiation

Thanks, I have the [payment] and [term]. Could you break down the [amount] due at signing into first payment, customer down payment, fees and refundable deposits? Please also confirm [one remaining detail, such as the loaner's starting odometer or the eligibility requirement for a rebate]. That will let me compare this one fairly.

## Counter using a real alternative

Here's a fictional version using the [worked example](field-guide.md). Use actual numbers from a current written quote when you send it.

I like this car. I have a local offer on the same trim with the audio package at $550 including tax, first payment only at signing, for 36 months and 10k miles a year. With the $900 delivery quote, $525 a month on those same terms would match it for me. If you can do that, please send the updated worksheet and confirm the car is available.

**If the competing car has different equipment**, drop the price-match claim. Say what you value directly: “The other car is less expensive, but yours has the audio package I want. I can make [amount] work with [exact cash due, term and miles]. Is that possible on this stock number?”

## Confirm before the trip

I'm ready to arrange [pickup/delivery] for stock [number]. Please send the final worksheet confirming [payment including tax], [term and miles], [cash due at signing and what it covers], and [any separate fees or shipping]. Please confirm the [required option], current odometer and how long the quote is good for. If a hold requires a deposit, please send its amount, refund terms and how it is applied to the signing cash.
